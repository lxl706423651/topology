# Factor Source Notes

- Momentum and relative-strength inspiration: Jegadeesh and Titman (1993), "Returns to Buying Winners and Selling Losers".
- Low-volatility and downside-risk inspiration: Ang, Hodrick, Xing, and Zhang (2006), "The Cross-Section of Volatility and Expected Returns".
- Amihud illiquidity inspiration: Amihud (2002), "Illiquidity and Stock Returns".
- Range-based volatility inspiration: Parkinson (1980), Garman-Klass-style estimators, and later range-volatility estimator reviews.
- Formulaic price-volume transformations and correlation-aware alpha construction inspiration: Kakushadze (2016), "101 Formulaic Alphas".

All implemented features are rewritten against the local OHLCV/amount schema and use only current or historical completed bars.
