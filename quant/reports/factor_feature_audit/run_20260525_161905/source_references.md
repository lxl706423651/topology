# 因子来源说明

- 动量和相对强弱因子参考：Jegadeesh and Titman (1993), "Returns to Buying Winners and Selling Losers", DOI `10.1111/j.1540-6261.1993.tb04702.x`。
- 低波动和下行风险因子参考：Ang, Hodrick, Xing, and Zhang (2006), "The Cross-Section of Volatility and Expected Returns", DOI `10.1111/j.1540-6261.2006.00836.x`。
- Amihud 非流动性因子参考：Amihud (2002), "Illiquidity and Stock Returns", DOI `10.1016/S1386-4181(01)00024-6`。
- Parkinson 价格区间波动率参考：Parkinson (1980), "The Extreme Value Method for Estimating the Variance of the Rate of Return", DOI `10.1086/296071`。
- Garman-Klass 价格区间波动率参考：Garman and Klass (1980), "On the Estimation of Security Price Volatilities from Historical Data", DOI `10.1086/296072`。
- 公式化价量变换和低相关 alpha 构造参考：Kakushadze (2016), "101 Formulaic Alphas", arXiv `1601.00991`。

本仓库中的实现已按本地 OHLCV/amount 字段重写，所有已实现特征只使用当前及历史已完成 bar。
